A note on coverage

The Global South isn't an afterthought here — it's the angle.

Most AI governance writing comes from Brussels, London, or Washington. This toolkit is built from the opposite direction: from someone practicing in Islamabad, working across South Asian and Gulf legal frameworks, and asking what the EU AI Act actually means when the regulator is the FIA, not the ICO.

How I Got Here

Essay

Two degrees, one small-town courtroom, and no one to call

An LL.B in Faisalabad and a data science degree over video calls — two enrollments that made no sense side by side, until a small law chamber in Samundri, still run entirely on paper, taught me the sequence I now argue for: digitize first, audit what you find, then automate. This is the story of the years in between.
